# Site copy inventory

Every piece of visible copy on **websitesbymanny.netlify.app**, in the order a
visitor meets it, with the file and field it comes from and what job it does.

Generated by `tools/copy-inventory.mjs`. Regenerate after any copy change.

---

## Before you rewrite anything

**What this site is.** A personal portfolio for Manny Pachano, a front-end
developer and marketing specialist. It is read by prospective clients and
employers, and it doubles as a record of his work history. It is set to
`noindex`, so it is not a search asset. Its only job is to be accurate and
believed.

**The single biggest failure mode.** Previous audits of this copy invented
things: a client engagement that did not exist, a contact section that was
never on the site, and landing page descriptions that quietly dropped the
lines disclosing the work was unsolicited. Do not describe anything you cannot
see in the copy in front of you.

## Hard rules

1. **Invent nothing.** Every figure, client name, date, technology and scale
   claim here is real. Do not add a metric, a result, a tool or an outcome that
   is not already in the text you were given.

2. **Never delete a disclosure.** Three landing pages are unsolicited concept
   redesigns of real organizations, and two are invented businesses. Phrases
   like "built without being asked", "Everything on it is theirs", "an invented
   firm", "none of the figures are real" and "labeled illustrative" are
   load-bearing. Removing them turns a concept piece into a false claim of paid
   work. If a rewrite loses one, the rewrite is wrong.

3. **Do not trade specific for generic.** "A used oil collection company" must
   not become "an environmental services company". "The nine styles he teaches"
   must not become "lesson descriptions". "Suwanee, Georgia" must not become
   "Georgia". The specifics are the value.

4. **Keep the first person.** This is written as Manny, not as a CV. "I built
   it on Astro", not "Built on Astro". No resume verbs: no spearheaded,
   re-architected, engineered, leveraged, utilized.

5. **No unverifiable claims.** No high-converting, lightning-fast,
   industry-leading, world-class, seamless, cutting-edge.

## Voice

- Literal and plain. Say what the thing is and what it does.
- No em dashes and no en dashes. Write ranges as "10 to 20".
- No metaphors, no aphorisms, no sentence that ends on a flourish. If a
  sentence reads like it was written to be quoted, cut it.
- No exclamation marks. No cliches.
- Short sentences. A non-technical reader should follow every line.
- These are captions in a fixed layout. Stay within about ten percent of the
  original length unless you are cutting.

## Two mechanical notes

- Some fields allow inline `<strong>` and `<a>`. Keep the tags unless the
  emphasis genuinely belongs elsewhere.
- Every mention of Clox sits behind one flag (`SHOW_CLOX` in `src/config.ts`)
  so it can be removed site-wide in a single change. Do not add Clox to any
  file that does not already import that flag.

## How to return your edits

For each change, give three things and nothing else:

```
FILE → FIELD PATH   (copy it exactly as printed in this document)
OLD: <the original string, verbatim>
NEW: <the replacement>
```

Do not return rewritten files, and do not renumber or reorder anything.

**Flag rather than fix:** a claim that looks factually wrong, a date that
conflicts with another page, or an inconsistency between two sections. Say so
in a list at the end. Do not guess at a correction.
